Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This policy covers "Postiz Schoppe," a private publishing tool operated by The Golf Schop (John Schoppe) to draft and schedule posts to The Golf Schop's own social media accounts, including TikTok, Instagram and X.

What we collect

When an account owner connects a social account, the tool receives the access tokens and basic profile information (such as display name, username and avatar) that the platform provides for the permissions granted. It also stores the posts, captions and media the owner chooses to publish.

How we use it

This information is used only to show which account is connected and to upload, draft and publish content to that account at the owner's direction. We do not sell, rent or share this information with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

Storage and security

Data is stored on a privately hosted server controlled by the operator and is accessible only to the operator.

Retention and deletion

Tokens and data are kept only while the account remains connected. Disconnecting the account in the tool, or revoking access in the platform's settings, removes access. To request deletion, email schop@thegolfschop.com.
